Why we built CLIMAS

A safety device made for the real dangers in a Ghanaian home, by people who live with them too.

Two things quietly put homes at risk here, and most houses have no way to detect either.

The first is cooking gas. Almost every kitchen runs on an LPG cylinder, and a worn hose or a regulator left slightly open can fill a room while everyone sleeps. By the time you smell it, there can already be enough in the air to catch a spark.

The second is carbon monoxide. When the power goes and a generator runs too close to the house, it produces a gas with no smell and no colour. People have gone to sleep with the generator running and not woken up. It happens often enough that everyone knows a story.

Imported detectors exist, but they are usually built for one problem, priced for another country, and go silent the moment they lose power, which here is exactly when you need them most. So we built CLIMAS around how we actually live: both gases in one device, a loud alarm that does not need wifi or a subscription, and a battery so it keeps watching through dumsor.

CLIMAS is designed and built in Kumasi. The alarm is yours to keep and always works. The subscription only adds the connected features that genuinely need our servers, like calling your phone when you are not home. We keep that line honest on purpose, because a safety device should never go quiet over a late payment.

We are still early, and building carefully, because this is not the kind of product you rush. If you want to be part of it, the best thing you can do is get a device and tell us what would make it better.
