Two real dangers, one device

The things that fill a home quietly

Most homes here have no way to know either of these is happening until it is already dangerous. CLIMAS is built specifically for both.

LPG cooking gas

A cylinder that leaks while you sleep

A loose regulator or a worn hose lets cooking gas fill the room slowly. You often cannot smell it until there is enough to catch a spark.

CLIMAS senses LPG building up and sounds the alarm early, while opening a window and closing the valve is still all it takes.

Carbon monoxide

A generator running through the night

When the lights go and the generator comes on too close to the house, it produces carbon monoxide. It has no smell and no colour, and people go to sleep and do not wake up.

CLIMAS detects carbon monoxide directly and calls your phone, so someone knows even if everyone inside is already asleep.
